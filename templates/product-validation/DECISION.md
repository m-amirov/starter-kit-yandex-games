# Concept Proof Decision

## Kill criteria

Mark each as met or not met:

- first action is not understood within 10 seconds;
- oral explanation is required;
- player does not understand the cause of success or error;
- core loop cannot be described in one sentence;
- added depth requires another interface section instead of deepening the field;
- at least two independent players stop in the opening minutes.

Any met criterion blocks `CONTINUE_PRODUCTION` until a documented product-owner
exception or a new blind-playtest result resolves it.
