# Blind Playtest

## Preconditions

- Tester has not read the game spec.
- Tester has not seen development prompts.
- Tester receives no oral explanation.
- Tester starts with a clean save.
- Observer does not coach, correct, or explain.

## Session

Record timestamps and ask only after observation:

1. What are you supposed to do here?
2. How did you understand the first move?
3. Why was the decision correct?
4. What happened after an error?
5. What changed after success?
6. When did it become unclear?
7. When did it become boring?
8. Did you want to continue?
9. How would you describe the game in one sentence?
