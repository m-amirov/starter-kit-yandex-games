# Quality Constitution

## Purpose

The starter kit is intended to produce commercially presentable small and medium HTML5 games, not merely compilable prototypes. Automated development may remove intermediate human approval, but it does not lower the product or visual bar.

## Distinctiveness requirement

Every game must define a specific combination of:

- player fantasy;
- mechanical signature;
- visual thesis;
- tactile interaction signature;
- progression promise.

A game fails this requirement when its theme, UI and effects could be exchanged with another generated game without changing its identity.

## Required production passes

### 1. Product thesis

Define audience, fantasy, session structure, core decision, failure/recovery pattern and why the game deserves another session.

### 2. Vertical slice

Build one polished representative slice before producing all levels or assets. Validate controls, readability, pacing and the visual language.

### 3. Content production

Generate content only after the slice passes. Use authored constraints, validation and playtesting rather than uncontrolled bulk generation.

### 4. Integration polish

Review the complete UI and gameplay hierarchy at real viewports. Fix spacing, focus, feedback, animation timing, copy and asset consistency.

### 5. Independent final review

Re-run the product and visual reviews against the final production build.

## Release-blocking quality failures

- core gameplay is repetitive before the intended session ends;
- tutorial explains controls but not decisions;
- visual identity is generic or internally inconsistent;
- UI resembles a generic dashboard unrelated to the game world;
- feedback is missing, delayed or visually noisy;
- levels are generated without validation or meaningful progression;
- meta systems exist only to increase screen count;
- marketing visuals exaggerate or replace actual gameplay;
- final screenshots contain overflow, overlaps, unreadable text or empty composition;
- the agent marks its own first asset pass as final without integrated review.

See `.codex/skills/product-quality-review/SKILL.md` and `.codex/skills/visual-quality-gate/SKILL.md`.


## Production skill gates (0.4.x)

A high-quality vertical slice and final production build are reviewed through product, visual, game-feel, mobile, accessibility and performance gates. Level, audio and asset-rights gates apply when those systems are present. These gates may be stricter than platform minimums but cannot conflict with or replace Yandex compliance.
